Losses: a recorded loss lands on the first packet stored after it and never on packets already there, waits when nothing follows yet, drops a packet split across it, survives an empty split packet, adds up with a dropped-packet reason, and does nothing for SMB or unknown writers; a refused non-empty batch counts as a loss the same way. Refusals: each abi_violations and chunks_discarded case the refusals bullet names, an empty batch, and mixed formats on one writer ID both ways including the patch path, none of them evicting stored data. Overwrite and clone: overwritten batches show on the survivors, an overwrite and a pending loss both reach the next packet, and a clone keeps losses not yet read.
